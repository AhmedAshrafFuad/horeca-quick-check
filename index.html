Product list UX

Add the first unit in one tap

What happens now

Before a product is in the cart, the card already shows minus, plus, and a cart button. Adding the first unit takes two taps: plus, then the cart icon.

What to change

One tap on plus adds the product. Hide minus until the quantity is at least 1. After that, minus and plus only change the amount. A second cart tap is not required.

Product details Layout

Give the price room above Add to Cart

What happens now

The total and the crossed-out price sit on the quantity row. In this screen they are also tucked under the sticky search bar, so “TOTAL: EGP 990.00” and “WAS: EGP 975” are cut off.

What to change

Keep a clear gap between the price block and the buttons. Keep the price fully visible below the header, so scrolling never slides it under the search field.

Product list Layout

Show the quantity on the product card

What happens now

The number between minus and plus never appears. The cart icon and “Total: EGP 0.00” take the whole row, and the total itself is clipped. On Home, the same control does show 0, so the quantity exists. The list card is just too narrow.

What to change

Give the stepper room for minus, the number, and plus. Move the line total onto its own line when the card is narrow.

Same row as issue 01. After the first tap adds the item, the card still needs room to show the quantity.

Previous orders UX

A guest should see login, not a list title

What happens now

Orders opens a screen titled “Previous orders”, with a back arrow. The body is empty except “Please Login First” and a Login button.

What to change

If the shopper is not logged in, show the login prompt on its own. Show the Previous orders title only when there is an order list to open.

All screens UX

Search does not belong on every screen

What happens now

Logo, search, and the header cart are fixed on Home, lists, product details, and the login wall. On product details the search bar covers the price. On Previous orders, search is not the task.

What to change

Keep search on Home. From the other screens, open it from a Search tab in the bottom bar, or from a floating button. Those screens get a normal title bar instead.

Product details Layout

Pad the shipping and support block

What happens now

Trusted Shipping, Secure Payment, Call Us, and Email Support have no side padding. The shipping sentence is cut at “restaurant”, and “cafes and companies” drops onto the next line.

What to change

Use the same horizontal padding as the Add to Cart row. Let the full sentence wrap inside the row, with space between the icon and the text.

Navigation Bug

Back from the product list breaks the screen

What happens now

  • Going into a product list and back does not return to one screen.
  • The previous page stays up during the animation. For a moment there are two headers: two logos and two search fields, with product columns from more than one list side by side.
  • Another back gesture flashes a half-drawn category screen. Only the Smart Offers card is there, its image is blank, and the product list is still visible underneath on the left.

What to change

Back pops a single screen and shows the category the shopper came from. One header, one page, and nothing left underneath.

Home, categories, product list Bug

Cache images with the products

What happens now

Names and prices are already cached. Images are not. On launch the cards draw first and the photos pop in a moment later. Going back, photos fade to empty and load again. Offline, the text is still there and every photo is a blank box.

What to change

Store images on disk with the product cache, keyed by image URL. Show the stored photo immediately, including when the phone is offline. Do not download an image that is already saved.

Launch with no connection Bug

Say when the phone is offline

What happens now

With cellular data off, the app opens Home from the cache and looks like a normal session, except the photos are missing. There is no offline screen and no banner inside the app. The only message is the iPhone alert “Cellular Data is Turned Off”. Tapping Add to Cart then fails with an error, and nothing explains that the phone is offline.

What to change

  • As soon as the app knows there is no connection, say so on screen.
  • Cached names and prices can stay browsable.
  • If Add to Cart cannot finish, say that in the app: the item was not added because there is no connection.
